## 5. Current Completion Percentage — Not Historical Say-Do

The required formula is:

> (Known Story Points of Currently Completed Stories / Total Known Story Points of Stories Currently Assigned to the Iteration) × 100

Constraints:

- Completed states must be “explicitly confirmed” by workflow mapping.
- The JSON does not provide any mapping of which states (e.g., `Resolved`, `Dev-Deployed`, `Ready for QA`) are considered completed.
- Many stories within each iteration have **missing Story Points** (`null`).

Because:

1. **No completed-state mapping** is provided in the JSON, and  
2. Several iterations have a mix of estimated and unestimated stories,

it is **not possible** to safely compute valid Current Completion Percentage values per iteration.

**Result:** For all iterations, the Current Completion Percentage is **“Insufficient Data”** due to:

- Missing state-to-completion mapping.
- Presence of unestimated stories (null Story Points) in iterations.

## 14. Final Product Manager Summary

Overall, Azure DevOps planning data for the **AAVA Integrations** team is **structurally complete** (all items present and linked) but **planning-light**:

- **Epics**: Only Priority is populated; value, effort, and schedule fields are entirely missing, limiting strategic decision-making.
- **Features**: Priority and Studio are strong; Effort, Target Date, Release Version, and Complexity are partially populated; Primary User Cohort is unmapped for all Features.
- **User Stories**: Priority is consistently set, but Story Points are missing on about **two-thirds** of stories, and dates, release versions, and dependent teams are rarely filled.

Current Completion Percentage per iteration **cannot be calculated** from the JSON because:

- There is **no explicit mapping** from workflow states (e.g., `Resolved`, `Dev-Deployed`) to a “Completed” definition.
- Many iterations (particularly **PI3 Sprint 4** and **PI4 Sprint 2**) contain a mix of estimated and unestimated stories, so total committed Story Points are unknown.

Major delivery planning concerns:

- High volume of **unestimated work** in active PI sprints (PI3, PI4), weakening capacity planning and predictability.
- Lack of Epic-level planning fields impedes **portfolio alignment** and trade-off discussions.
- Incomplete Feature planning (Effort, Target Date, Release Version) complicates **release-level planning** and stakeholder communication.
- Bug and technical stories often have no Story Points, reducing visibility into **quality and technical debt** workload.

Three highest-priority corrective actions:

1. **Establish and document completed-state mapping** in Azure DevOps, then update analytics to use that mapping.  
2. **Complete Story Point estimation** for all committed stories in **PI3 Sprint 4** and **PI4 Sprint 2**, and for new Pantheon/Unity work in **PI4 Sprint 6**.  
3. **Populate core planning fields on Epics and key Features** (Effort, Business Value, Time Criticality, Target Date, Release Version), enabling more reliable PI and release planning.

With these actions, the team can move from mostly qualitative backlog tracking to **quantitative, iteration-level predictability** while keeping planning data health at a level suitable for management reporting.
